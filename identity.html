Tagline & Mission▾
Connecting Sub-Saharan skies.Draft

Mission. VATSSA provides realistic, welcoming virtual air traffic control across Sub-Saharan Africa — training controllers to international standards, supporting pilots, and growing the region's flight-simulation community on the VATSIM network.

Tone of Voice▾

Sound like a calm, capable controller: precise, friendly, and inclusive — never stiff or jargon-heavy with newcomers.

Professional
Accurate and dependable — aviation-grade precision.
Welcoming
Encouraging to newcomers; assume good intent.
Clear
Short sentences, plain English, no needless jargon.
Pan-African
Inclusive of the whole region; respectful and proud.
