Logo Usage · Do & Don't▾

✓Do

  • Use the official files — SVG wherever possible.
  • Keep clear space around the logo.
  • White-disc icon on dark; gradient-fill tile on any background.
  • Dark wordmark on light, white wordmark on dark.
  • Preserve the teal→green gradient direction.

✕Don't

  • Don't stretch, squash or rotate the logo.
  • Don't recolour it or alter the gradient.
  • Don't add shadows, outlines or effects.
  • Don't place it on a busy / low-contrast background.
  • Don't use the old logo or low-res copies.
